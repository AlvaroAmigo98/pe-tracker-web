Capabilities
What it does
Weekly primary-source scrape
100+ fund team pages read every week. New names, missing names and title changes classified as hire, exit or promotion — no manual entry.
Cascade detection
A senior exit followed by colleagues within eight weeks is flagged as a cascade — the early signal of a team lift-out.
Person histories
Every individual's full trajectory — title by title, firm by firm, timestamped. Follow a career, not a snapshot.
Market Pulse
Net headcount, hire/exit ratio and 52-week momentum across the tracked universe, read at a glance.
Talent flow
See where people move between funds — which houses feed each other, and where the pipelines run.
Watchlists & export
Flag the firms you care about, get surfaced when they move, and pull any view to Excel or a firm report.
